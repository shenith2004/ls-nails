Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything to know before your first visit to our nail salon in Negombo. Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Where is LS Nails Studio in Negombo?

We're at Archbishop Nicholas Marcus Fernando Mawatha, Negombo 11500, Sri Lanka. We welcome clients from across Negombo and nearby Kochchikade, Katunayake, Seeduwa and Ja-Ela. Get directions on Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. We're closed on Sundays.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form and it opens WhatsApp with your request, or call or WhatsApp us on +94 71 208 1576. We keep bookings small so no one is rushed, so it's best to book ahead.

What nail services do you offer?

Gel and acrylic nail extensions, gel polish, pedicures and custom hand-painted nail art, from classic nudes and French tips to chrome, ombré, florals and 3D charms. Browse real sets from the studio in our nail art gallery.

Do you also do eyelash extensions and waxing?

Yes. We apply classic, hybrid and volume eyelash extensions strand by strand, and offer soft and hard wax hair removal, from eyebrow shaping to full body.

How long do gel and acrylic nail extensions last?

With good care, most sets look their best for about three to four weeks, after which we recommend coming back for maintenance or a fresh set. We'll talk you through aftercare at your appointment.

Can I bring my own nail design idea?

Of course. Add your idea to the booking notes or send us a reference photo on WhatsApp, and we'll recreate it or tailor it to your nail shape.

Is the studio hygienic?

Every tool is sanitised and every surface cleaned between clients, and we only use professional-grade, cruelty-free, skin-tested products.
